Guest charter

Guest Guidelines

Our guidelines exist to ensure every guest - including you - enjoys a comfortable, safe, and respectful stay.

Timing

Check-In & Check-Out

Check-in from 2:00 PM. Check-out by 12:00 Noon. Early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability and may incur additional charges.

Identity

Valid ID Proof

All guests must present a valid government-issued photo ID at check-in. Foreign nationals must provide a valid passport and visa.

Access

Guest Registration

Only registered guests are permitted to stay in the room. Visitors are allowed only in designated areas and during permitted hours.

Billing

Payment Policy

Full payment or advance payment is required at check-in. All additional charges incurred during the stay must be settled before check-out.

Conduct

No Smoking Policy

Smoking inside rooms and all indoor areas is strictly prohibited. The hotel maintains a clean, smoke-free environment throughout the property.

Booking

Cancellation & Refund

Cancellation and refund policies are subject to the booking terms agreed at the time of reservation. Please confirm cancellation terms at booking.

By checking into Urban Stay, the guest agrees to abide by all Terms & Conditions of the hotel.

Questions

Frequently Asked

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in starts at 2:00 PM and check-out is by 12:00 Noon. Early check-in and late check-out depend on availability and may incur an additional charge.

Is parking available at Urban Stay?

On-site parking is available for guests, subject to availability. Please contact the front desk if you need assistance.

Is Wi-Fi complimentary for all guests?

Yes. Complimentary Wi-Fi is available throughout the property.

What documents are required at check-in?

All guests must present a valid government-issued photo ID. Foreign nationals must also provide a valid passport and visa.

Are external visitors allowed inside rooms?

Only registered guests may stay in rooms. Visitors are permitted in designated areas and during approved visiting hours.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancellation and refund terms depend on the booking conditions agreed at reservation. Please check your booking confirmation or contact the hotel for details.
